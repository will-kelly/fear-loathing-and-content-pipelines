## What it is

Kestrel is an audit-log tool for engineering and security teams. It collects "who changed what, and when" from the tools a team already uses, puts it all in one place, and makes it searchable.

## The one-line description (use this wording)

"Kestrel answers who changed what, and who said they could."

## Who it's for

Platform engineering and security leads at companies with 200 to 5,000 employees. Usually they come to us before a compliance audit, or after an incident where nobody could find the change history.

## What it connects to

42 tools as of September 2026, including GitHub, GitLab, AWS CloudTrail, Okta, and Terraform Cloud. Anything else can send events through a webhook.

## What it is not

- Not a security monitoring tool. It doesn't detect threats.
- Not an approval tool. It records approvals that happened somewhere else.

## Security
