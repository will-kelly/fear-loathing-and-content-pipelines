# Step 1: the request

Dana from Sales wants a LinkedIn post. Here's what she sent first, and then what it took to make it a request.

## What Dana sent

> Can we get a LinkedIn post up about the SOC 2 thing? Mention we're certified and pricing. Also the ISO one if we can.

That's a topic, not a task. The AI's reply:

> Happy to. Before I draft, I need the request form, mostly the "Point" line: one sentence saying what a reader should believe after reading. "Something about SOC 2" could go fifty ways. Also a heads-up from the source file: it says to use "SOC 2 Type II audited," not "certified," and ISO 27001 is listed under "things we don't talk about yet." I can leave ISO out, or you can ask Priya whether it's time to add it.
