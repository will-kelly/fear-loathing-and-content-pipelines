## What the columns tell you

- **Requested to Shipped** is your turnaround time. After ten rows you'll know your real number, and it'll be longer than the drafting time by a lot. That gap is the thing to fix next, and it's never the AI.
- **Rounds** is how many times the checklist came back with a no. If it's always 1, the checklist might be too easy. If it's always 3, the source file or the request form needs work.
- **Approved by** is the name that goes with the piece. If the wrong price ships, this is who finds out first.
- **Source version** tells you which pieces to re-check when the source file changes. If Priya bumps it to 2 because the price changed, every row that says 1 and mentions price needs a look.
- **Notes** is where the interesting stuff ends up. A stalled row with a reason is worth more than a shipped row without one.
