# Kestrel pricing and packaging (2026)

Effective October 1, 2026. This file replaces the 2025 pricing sheet. The 2025 plan names (Starter, Growth, Scale) no longer exist.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Team | $400/month, billed annually | Up to 10 sources, 1 year retention, 5 seats, email support |
| Business | $1,800/month, billed annually | Up to 50 sources, 3 years retention, 25 seats, SSO, priority support |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited sources, up to 7 years retention, unlimited seats, dedicated support, custom data residency |

## Add-ons

- Additional retention beyond the plan limit: $0.02 per GB per month.
- Additional seats on Team and Business: $25 per seat per month.

## Trial

14-day free trial on the Team plan. No credit card required. Trial accounts keep their data if they convert within 30 days of trial end.

## What we don't say

- We do not publish per-event pricing. There isn't any.
- We do not quote discounts in content. Discounts are handled by Sales.
- We do not promise price locks beyond the current contract term.

## Approved price phrasing

- "Starts at $400 a month on an annual plan."
- "Business plans are $1,800 a month, billed annually."
- "Enterprise pricing is custom."
