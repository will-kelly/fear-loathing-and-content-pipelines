# Northfield Labs style guide

The voice auditor enforces this file. Anything listed under "banned" is a hard fail at Gate 3.

## Voice

- Plain and direct. Write like an engineer explaining something to another engineer over coffee.
- Short sentences. If a sentence has two "and"s, split it.
- Say the thing once. Don't restate a claim for emphasis.
- Specific beats impressive. "42 integrations" beats "dozens of integrations." "Under 2 seconds" beats "lightning fast."
- We don't talk about ourselves in the third person. "We" and "Kestrel," never "Northfield Labs is proud to."

## Banned words and phrases

revolutionary, game-changing, seamless, seamlessly, unlock, unleash, empower, leverage (as a verb), robust, cutting-edge, next-generation, best-in-class, world-class, synergy, holistic, delve, journey (as in "your compliance journey"), "in today's fast-paced world," "it's no secret that," "at the end of the day"

## Banned patterns

- Rhetorical questions as openers ("Ever wondered who changed that config?").
- Triplets for rhythm ("faster, safer, smarter").
- Em-dash chains. One em dash per paragraph at most; prefer a period.
- "Not just X, but Y."
- Exclamation points.
- Starting a sentence with "Imagine."

## Claims

- Every number carries its qualifier from `customer-proof.md`.
- Security claims use the approved phrasing from `security-faq.md` and nothing stronger.
- Prices use the approved phrasing from `pricing-and-packaging.md`.

## Formatting by channel

| Channel | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn post | 120 to 200 words | No hashtags in the body. Up to three at the end. One link, in the first comment, not the body. |
| Blog post | 600 to 1,200 words | H2s only, no H3s. One image minimum. |
| Email | 80 to 150 words | One call to action. Subject line under 50 characters. |
| Release note | 50 to 120 words per item | Lead with what changed, then why. |

## Sentence case

Titles and headings are sentence case. "Kestrel pricing and packaging," not "Kestrel Pricing and Packaging."
